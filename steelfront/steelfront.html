Spotted enemies (seen by you or an ally, or just fired near your side) get a red marker with their range, a red arrow at the screen edge when off-screen and a red square on the minimap; a red arc around the crosshair points to whoever just hit you. The small circle shows where your gun is really pointed; over an enemy it turns green (shell gets through), yellow (maybe), red (no) or grey (ricochet). Angle your front armour, hit enemy sides & rear, shoot tracks to immobilise. Penetrations knock out crew: lose all but one and the tank is lost. APHE wrecks crews, AP gets through thicker plate, HE is for open-topped vehicles.

Aircraft spawn in the air behind your base. Point the mouse where you want to go and the plane follows. Easy flight (on by default, L toggles) stops stalls, pulls up before the ground and keeps climbs and dives gentle. Set your speed with W/S or the 1–4 presets; the throttle bar shows the setting, the blue tick the speed it settles at and the red tick the stall speed. The cross is where your guns point; lead moving targets using the lead marker. The diamond on the ground is where a bomb would land.
